\hQuick Race\n\nCompete in a randomly selected race using a variety of cars.\n\n\hArcade\n\nWelcome to the world of pink slip racing. Begin as a Rookie and work your way through some serious race events to become the race King! To unlock tracks, you need to win races, to win races you need to put your car on the line by betting its pink slip against one of your opponents. Win and you get his car, lose and your ride is lost. \n
\hControls\n\n2/Up - Change Gear (Drag mode only)\n\n4/Left - Steer left *\n\n6/Right - Steer right *\n\n8/Down - Brake/Reverse *\n\n5/Fire - Nitro\n\n0 - Camera View\n\nPause soft key - Pauses game\n\nNote: In Drag Races your car steers automatically but you have to change gear and Nitro.\n\n* Does not apply to drag races\n

HINT
Use the pink slip option to bet your ride against an opponent. Win and his car is yours. Lose and your ride is history!
Car stats dont count Nitro. Some slower cars can house large tanks of Nitro! Great drivers can win a race with ANY car!
In a road race, the blue arrows indicate a gentle corner and the red indicates a hard corner. Depending on your car, you may need to brake for red corners!
Look ahead to your upcoming races. They may help you decide which opponent is best to pink slip against!
All drift tracks have a drift target to reach! You can't win if you don't hit the target.
In a race, you can spot the opponent you bet against by the pink slip above his car!
In a drag race, time your nitro boost properly to make more than one boost per race!
Muscle cars are so powerful they dont need Nitro; however, there is one that holds a secret!
In a drag race, you should only change gear when the needle turns green!
If you're busted in a cop chase, your ride will be impounded!
